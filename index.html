How it works

From idea to finished piece

  1. 1

    Tell me your idea

    Send a message with what you have in mind, a rough size and where it will live. Photos and sketches are welcome.

  2. 2

    Agree the details

    We settle on the design, timber, finish and price before any wood is cut, so there are no surprises.

  3. 3

    Made by hand

    Your piece is built in the workshop, with updates along the way if you would like them.

  4. 4

    Collect or deliver

    Pick up locally in Prescot, or get in touch about delivery for larger pieces.

About Phil

A small workshop, on purpose

The Fat Hamster Wood Shop is Phil Smith's workshop in Prescot. Working at a small scale means every commission gets proper attention, and every customer deals directly with the person making their piece. That goes for local bars and businesses as well as homes.

The name? Take a look at the logo. Some things are just better when they are a bit chunky and well fed.

Made by one pair of hands, for one person's home.
